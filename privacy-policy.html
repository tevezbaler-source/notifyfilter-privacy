Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: January 2025

NotifyFilter does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All data remains exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Collect

NotifyFilter operates entirely on your device. The app processes the following data locally only:

  • Notification content — titles and text of notifications from other apps, used solely to match your keywords.
  • Keywords — words you enter to filter notifications, stored locally via AsyncStorage.
  • App settings — preferences such as language, speech rate, and car mode, stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

All processing happens on-device:

  • Notification text is scanned in real time against your keywords.
  • Matched notifications are saved to a local SQLite database on your device.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS) reads matched notifications aloud using your device's built-in engine.
  • No data is transmitted to any server or third party.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third party. NotifyFilter has no backend server and makes no network requests related to your personal data.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notification Listener — to read incoming notifications and filter them by your keywords.
  • Bluetooth — to detect car kit connection for automatic Car Mode activation.
  • Foreground Service — to keep the notification listener running reliably in the background.
  • Internet — standard React Native requirement; not used to transmit personal data.

5. Data Retention

Notification history is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. You can clear this data at any time from the History tab within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

NotifyFilter is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted externally, your information is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
